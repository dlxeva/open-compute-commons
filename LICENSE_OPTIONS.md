# License Options: Decision Record

> **Decision made.** This repository uses a single MIT License for all files.
> The full text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), and the scope is stated in `README.md`,
> `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `PUBLISH_CHECKLIST.md`.

## What the decision resolved

Licensing has consequences that outlast this draft. The repository is published as
`dlxeva/open-compute-commons` from a personal account. No legal entity exists behind the project,
and no institution is claimed as a rights holder.

The MIT License applies uniformly to documentation, code, JSON Schemas, machine-readable fixtures,
validation scripts, examples, and language mirrors. A single license keeps the reuse boundary clear
across the repository and avoids file-type classification.

## Why MIT

- It permits reuse, modification, distribution, sublicensing, and commercial use.
- It is short and widely recognized.
- It includes the copyright and permission notices required for redistribution.
- Its warranty and liability language is included in the standard text in `LICENSE`.

The license choice covers the current candidate repository. It does not make any claim about the
existence, operation, maturity, endorsement, or adoption of the proposed system.
